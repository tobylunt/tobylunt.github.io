I had been wanting to work with cherry for a while, but somehow never really had, at least not on any meaningful project. In the US we are blessed with an abundant supply of domestic hardwoods - most notably walnut, oak, maple, and cherry. These woods are all delightful in their own way, each with their own strengths and weaknesses.

I also have been gradually improving the usability of my house's small sun room. One of the missing pieces in the room was comfy seating. So I decided to build seating out of cherry.
